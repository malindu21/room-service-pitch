The problem today

Ordering by phone or message works, until it gets busy.

Guests call or message with their room number and order. Staff write it down, pass it to the kitchen and confirm it by hand.

Today

"Room 7 here, 2 lime juice + 1 fried rice pls"
  • Room numbers and dishes get misheard or written down wrong.
  • Orders get lost between calls, messages and other requests.
  • Guests don't know if anyone has seen the order, so they call or message again.
  • Staff spend time on the phone instead of cooking and serving.

With the app

Order #5727 · Room 07 · #23 ×1, #1 ×2 · "less spicy"
  • The room is set by the QR code, so it is always correct.
  • Every order arrives complete: items, quantities, notes and total.
  • Guests see each step live, so there are no "is my food coming?" messages.
  • Dishes keep your menu numbers, so the kitchen recognises every order.
How it works for guests

Three steps, about thirty seconds.

These are screenshots from the working demo app.

  1. 1Scan and browse

    The QR code opens the menu for that room, with a photo of every dish, categories, search, and the same item numbers as your printed menu.

  2. 2Confirm the order

    Guests review items, add a note like "less spicy", and tap Confirm order. It goes straight to the kitchen.

  3. 3Follow it live

    A tracking screen shows each step with a tick and the time, ending with a small celebration when the food arrives.

A closer look

Food that sells itself.

Tapping a dish opens a large photo with its description, so guests can see exactly what they are ordering, which makes it easier to try something new, like a kottu or a watalappan.

No QR code handy?

Guests can pick their room instead.

If a guest opens the app another way, for example from a link you share at check-in, they choose their room from a simple grid before seeing the menu. The phone remembers it for the rest of their stay, and they can change it any time.

  • Works for every room you have
  • The room is confirmed before every order
  • Scanning a room's QR code skips this step
