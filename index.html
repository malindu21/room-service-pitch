How it works

From QR code to food at the door, in 6 simple steps.

  1. 01

    A QR card is placed in every room

    Each card belongs to one room. Room 7 gets the Room 7 card, so the system always knows where the order comes from.

    ROOM 07
    Scan to order food
  2. 02

    The guest scans it with the phone camera

    Any phone works, Android or iPhone. There is nothing to download and no account to create.

    CameraQR
    07
    Room 07 menuOpen
  3. 03

    Your menu opens, with the room number already filled in

    Photos, prices and the same item numbers as your printed menu. The guest never types the room number.

  4. 04

    The guest picks food and confirms

    They can add a note like “less spicy”. One tap on Confirm sends the order to your kitchen.

  5. 05

    The guest sees every step, live

    Accepted, preparing, ready. No more “Is my food coming?” calls to reception.

  6. 06

    The food is served to the room

    Every order arrives complete, with the right dishes, to the right room.

Why it is better

Today, and with QR ordering.

Today

  • Guests call or message reception to order
  • Room numbers and dishes get misheard
  • Staff write every order down by hand
  • Guests call again to ask where their food is

With QR ordering

  • Guests order from their room in about a minute
  • The room number is always correct
  • Every order arrives complete, with notes
  • Guests can see the progress, so fewer calls
